# Code of conduct

This project is a place to build a GTK 4 binding for PHP. Everything below follows from that.

## What is expected

- **Talk about the work.** Review the patch, the API, the failing test. Not the person who wrote it.
- **Assume competence and good faith.** Someone who does something differently usually had a reason;
  ask what it was before deciding it was carelessness.
- **Be direct and be kind at once.** "This leaks a reference on the error path, see `core/object`" is
  useful. "Did you even read the docs?" is not, and neither is agreeing with everything.
- **Accept a no.** A maintainer may decline a feature, an approach or a patch; the reasoning belongs in
  the thread, and repeating the request louder does not change it.
- **Stay on topic.** Issues and pull requests are about php-gtk4. Politics, recruiting and advertising
  are not.

## What is not tolerated

Harassment in any form: insults, demeaning or sexualised remarks, attacks on someone's identity —
including their gender, sexuality, disability, appearance, ethnicity, nationality, religion or age.
Publishing anyone's private information. Following someone across threads to keep at them. Deliberately
wasting people's time, and any behaviour whose point is to make someone feel unwelcome.

This applies to everything under this repository — issues, pull requests, code review, commit messages,
discussions — and to conduct elsewhere that spills into it.

## Enforcement

Report to <pohl@apsservices.de>. Reports go to the maintainer alone; say what happened, where, and what
you would like to see done. You will get an answer, and what you sent stays between us — nothing is
forwarded to the person you are reporting without asking you first.

Depending on what happened, the response is a private word, an edited or deleted comment, a warning, or a
block from the repository. Severity and repetition decide which; a first misstep that is put right is not
a ban. If you think a decision about you was wrong, say so in reply — it will be looked at again.

The maintainer is bound by this document like everyone else.
