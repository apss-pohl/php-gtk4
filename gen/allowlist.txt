# Layout managers (out of band, PLAN.md §3 wave 1's leftover): the object GTK 4 delegates allocation to. A widget that has a
# layout manager never reaches WidgetClass.size_allocate/measure (VfuncTest pins that), so
# without GtkLayoutManager a PHP subclass of GtkBox or GtkOverlay cannot influence its own
# layout at all - vfunc_allocate()/vfunc_measure() on a manager is the way in, and
